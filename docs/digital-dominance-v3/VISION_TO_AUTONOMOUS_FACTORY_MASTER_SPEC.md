## Vision workflow: deterministic steps
1. VISION INTAKE: business model, service, geography, customer, offer, compliance/fulfillment, brand, existing content, pricing and spending ceilings, account/tenant.
2. GOD MODE RESEARCH: use permissioned current external sources and real evidence for demand, competition, lead economics, margin, ranking difficulty, buyer intent and trends. Unknown scores stay UNKNOWN. Document 3+ comparable systems before any new visual design.
3. STRATEGY TOURNAMENT: exactly 10 independently differentiated strategies. Each strategy must have target buyer, positioning, revenue model, keyword/search-intent clusters, offer, funnel UX, local/industry differentiation, acquisition channels, fulfillment, compliance, 90-day hypothesis, evidence URLs, confidence, cost and measurable acceptance KPI. Assign a scored rank with transparent weights and confidence.
4. GPT DESIGN TOURNAMENT: for each of the 10 strategies, send an authenticated typed packet to GPT-compatible execution surface. Receive exactly 10 discrete versioned Web Packs/mockups with HTML preview, tokens, route map, components, page content, unique hero, CTA, forms, responsive states, citations/source facts and evidence. Never invent testimonials or local presence. Enforce exact visually distinct designs rather than recolors.
5. PREVIEW/APPROVAL: render and browser-test 10 distinct options. Let operator choose 1 master design plus explicitly approved complementary components. Lock visual version, screenshot reference, tokens and manifest.
6. BUILD CLIENT BUSINESS OS: responsive client portal, admin/ops portal, optional staff portal, CRM/lead inbox, quote/booking/follow-up, digital business card and vCard/QR, opt-in AI chat, accessibility/privacy/consent notices, social creative drafts/calendar, lead attribution and role-scoped analytics.
7. AGENT SWARM: coordinator, market research, strategy, UX, copy/SEO, engineering, data, CRM, social, testing/security, compliance and release validator. Each typed work packet has tenant, actor, scope, idempotency, approval, cost budget, retry/lease, rollback and receipt. GPT is orchestrated via verified API/connector, never implied to run persistently by itself.
8. PROGRAMMATIC FACTORY: generate sites and meaningful unique service/region pages from verified supply, service reach, ownership and first-party information. Hundreds of sites are a scaling target only after small canary validates differentiated value, Google search policy, indexing, user experience, lead quality, support load and unit economics. No doorway patterns, city-swaps, duplicate listings or ranking promises.
9. VALIDATE AND RELEASE: tests for build/lint/types/security/auth/RLS, data, forms, role separation, performance, accessibility, mobile/desktop, SEO, robots/noindex, tracking and conversion, functional end-to-end mock lead, rollback and independent QA. Previews noindex. Production releases, DNS, paid resources, credential changes and customer/public communications need scoped approval; automation resumes after approvals.
10. OPERATE/LEARN: use existing single heartbeat, queue and worker leases; monitor site/build health, search performance and first-party revenue attribution. Adjust experiment priorities; flag blocked/dead-letter items; route autonomous safe repairs with receipts.
